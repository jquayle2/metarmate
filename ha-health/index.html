Home Assistant Health

A private, single-household connection between Google Health and a self-hosted Home Assistant server.

Home Assistant Health is not a public app or a commercial service. It is the OAuth client that one family uses to let its own home automation server, running on hardware in the home, read sleep and activity data from a family member's Fitbit through the Google Health API.

The household uses that data to show on home dashboards whether she has had enough sleep, so the rest of the family knows when she needs to rest.

What it does

  • Reads sleep, activity and health-metric data, read-only, for the one account that authorized it.
  • Stores that data only on the household's own Home Assistant server.
  • Never writes to, changes or deletes anything in the user's Google account.

Who can use it

Only members of the developer's household. No one else can sign up, and the app is not offered or advertised to anyone.
